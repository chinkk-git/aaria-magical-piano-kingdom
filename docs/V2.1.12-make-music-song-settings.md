# V2.1.12 — Make Music dock + Song Settings star segment

Status: implementation pending. This document records owner-approved scope; it is not evidence of implemented UI.

## Owner request
Move **Make Music** out of the five-segment Magic Star to a dedicated, fixed left-side **3D component**. Replace the vacated star segment with **Song Settings**, opening a screen to edit song name and select song level.

## Requirements
1. Retain existing golden star, disco ball, highway, piano geometry, playback controls and remaining four Magic Star segments.
2. Left-side Make Music control: prominent, child-friendly 3D musical design, fixed without obscuring notes, keyboard, song selector, install, settings or controls; accessible on Android portrait/landscape, tablet and desktop; touch target, focus and reduced-motion support.
3. Make Music opens the **existing** creation/import workflow, without introducing a duplicate workflow.
4. Replace the former Make Music segment with **Song Settings** (five total). Song Settings opens a dedicated, responsive screen/dialog for current selected song.
5. Show full song name and Basic / Medium / Advanced selection. Allow rename for user-created/imported songs, persist after reload, and preserve IDs, notes, timing, arrangements, favorites, recent references, offline data. Shared bundled catalogue entries must not be mutated.
6. Display only actually available arrangements as selectable; unavailable levels disabled and explained. Switching levels must load corresponding arrangement rather than relabel notes. Do not fabricate arrangements or re-run OMR merely to switch.
7. Save/Cancel, validation and clear feedback; preserve existing song-selection and practice flow.
8. No new paid services, network dependencies or data loss.

## Acceptance and evidence
